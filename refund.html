Refund Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Try Quickey free first: the free version has every wheel and tool, with a daily limit. If you buy Pro and it isn't right for you, we'll refund you.

14-day money-back guarantee

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying Quickey Pro, for any reason. No questions asked.

How to ask for a refund

  • Reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or contact us using the details below, with your order number.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

After a refund

Your license key is deactivated, and Quickey goes back to the free version on your PCs. Your files and settings aren't affected.

After 14 days

We may still help in special cases, such as being charged twice or Quickey not running on your PC at all. Just get in touch.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/namastetechy-cell/quickey-releases/issues, or reply to the receipt email you got from Lemon Squeezy.
