Quickey

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Quickey is a Windows app that converts and edits files on your own PC. We built it so your files never leave your computer.

Your files

Every conversion, compression, crop and edit runs locally on your PC. Quickey never uploads, copies or looks at your files on any server. Conversions work with the internet switched off.

What the app sends over the internet

  • License activation and checks (Pro only). When you enter a license key, and about once a day afterwards, Quickey sends your license key, an activation ID and your PC's name to Lemon Squeezy's license service to confirm the key is valid. Nothing about your files is sent.
  • Update checks. Quickey asks GitHub whether a newer version exists and downloads it if so. GitHub sees a normal download request (such as your IP address), like any website visit.

Quickey has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Data kept on your PC

Settings, your daily conversion count, a log file for troubleshooting and your encrypted license are stored in %AppData%\Quickey on your PC. Uninstalling Quickey and deleting that folder removes them.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record. They process your name, email, billing details and payment information under their own privacy policy. We receive your email and order details so we can help with your license; we never see your full card details.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those services may log standard request data such as your IP address. The site sets no cookies of its own.

Your choices

You can ask us what purchase information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, using the contact below. Lemon Squeezy may need to keep some records for tax and legal reasons.

Children

Quickey is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/namastetechy-cell/quickey-releases/issues, or reply to the receipt email you got from Lemon Squeezy.
